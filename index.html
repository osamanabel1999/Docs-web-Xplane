🚀 What’s New in the Latest X-Plane Update

A major expansion focused on training, realism, and faster workflow—bringing advanced weather creation, TCAS practice, instant flight setup, detailed flight analysis, and deep aircraft systems monitoring directly to your device.

New Feature • Weather Training

Advanced Weather Control & Training Presets

Take full control of the atmosphere inside X-Plane with a far more powerful weather workflow. Instead of depending only on default conditions, you can now build complex training environments, fine-tune each weather element, and quickly switch between ready-made scenarios designed for practice.

1. Custom Weather Builder Create weather exactly the way you want it. Add multiple cloud layers and define each layer’s base altitude, top altitude, cloud type, coverage, turbulence, icing, precipitation type, and precipitation rate. You can also precisely control surface wind, wind aloft, temperature, pressure, visibility, and other atmospheric details to build highly specific training scenarios—from calm VFR days to demanding instrument conditions.

2. Weather Presets & Scenario Training Train faster using a wide range of built-in weather presets. Instantly load conditions such as Clear, VFR, VFR Few Clouds, VFR Scattered, Terrain Low Visibility, Snow Conditions, Thunderstorm, Icing Conditions, Microburst, Windshear, and Severe Turbulence. These presets make it easy to repeat challenging situations and practice handling abnormal weather in a structured, realistic way.

New Feature • Traffic Training

Interactive TCAS Trainer

Practice traffic awareness and conflict management with the new TCAS Trainer. This system allows you to manually inject traffic around your aircraft and create repeatable scenarios tailored for situational awareness and decision-making exercises.

1. Custom Traffic Injection Add any aircraft you want and define its distance, speed, vertical speed, separation, and relative direction from your aircraft, including North, South, East, West, Northeast, Northwest, Southeast, and Southwest. You can also assign a custom heading to create crossing traffic exactly the way you need it. The injected aircraft appears in the simulator, and if your aircraft supports TCAS on the ND, it will appear there as well—making this a highly effective training tool for real-time traffic scanning and TCAS interpretation.

New Feature • Flight Setup

Aircraft & Flight Selector

Launching a new flight is now dramatically faster. The new Aircraft & Flight Selector lets you build and load a complete X-Plane startup scenario directly from the app, without digging through the simulator’s setup menus.

1. Start a Flight in Seconds Choose the aircraft you want, search for any airport, and decide whether you want to begin at a gate/ramp or directly on a runway. The system displays the available gates and runways, allowing you to select the exact start position you need. You can also choose the desired startup state, such as Cold & Dark or engines running, making it much easier to begin training or operational flights in just a few seconds.

New Feature • Analysis

Flight Analysis, Personal Progress & Live Profiles

Turn every flight into a learning opportunity with the new Analysis tools. The app reviews important flight data, tracks recurring patterns across your flights, and presents your performance in a clear and structured way so you can improve with every session.

1. Personal Progress & Performance Scoring The Personal Progress page tracks your ongoing performance across all recorded flights. It reviews important data such as speed, altitude, handling trends, and other flight parameters to generate a meaningful long-term evaluation. Over time, the system builds a broader picture of your flying, giving you a score out of 100, highlighting patterns, and helping you understand where your strengths are and where you still need improvement.

2. Takeoff & Landing Analysis Review your takeoff and landing performance with far more precision. The system identifies the exact point where the aircraft became airborne or where it made touchdown, then displays useful data such as speed, groundspeed, runway distance, and other supporting parameters. A visual runway diagram marks the point of rotation or touchdown, while additional checks help analyze technique, including tendencies such as excessive bank or other handling issues during key phases of flight.

3. Live Profiles & Data Trends Explore live profile charts for important parameters such as airspeed, altitude, vertical speed, roll, and bank angle. These profiles make it easier to understand how the aircraft behaved throughout the flight and present the data in a cleaner, more readable way for detailed debriefing and self-analysis.

New Feature • Aircraft Systems

Deep Aircraft Systems Monitoring

The new Systems page gives you a deeper, more technical look into both the aircraft and simulator state. It brings together operational data, performance data, and system monitoring in one organized interface, making it much easier to inspect important information while flying.

1. Detailed Multi-Category Monitoring Browse a rich set of data pages such as Engines, Fuel, Systems, Alerts, and Data Monitor. On the Engine page, the app can display an extensive set of values for each available engine—whether the aircraft has 1, 2, or 4 engines. The result is a polished monitoring page designed for pilots who want quick access to detailed aircraft data.

X-Plane Connection Guide

Follow these exact steps to connect your device to X-Plane and gain full control.

Before you begin

  • Have X-Plane installed on your PC.
  • Keep your mobile device (iPad, Phone) and PC on the same Wi-Fi network.
  • If you use SimBrief, IVAO, or VATSIM, have your relevant ID ready.
  • Instructor Session: If you are flying in a shared session with an instructor, ensure both the Pilot and Instructor enter the exact same Session ID on the connection page.
Step 1

Download the Simulator Station Files

Download the Simulator Station files ZIP to your Windows PC. The package contains seven X-Plane plugin folders for the new direct installation method, plus optional tools for Instructor Operating Station screen sharing.

https://forums.x-plane.org/files/file/102136-simulator-station/

* Copy the link above and open it in your PC browser to download the files.

After downloading, extract the ZIP file to a convenient location on your PC. Inside the extracted folder, you will find one main folder: X-Plane. open the X-Plane folder.

Step 2

Install the 7 X-Plane Plugins

Connecting Simulator Station to X-Plane is now easier than ever. Our new plugin-based connection method replaces the old manual X-Plane Data Output configuration. Simply copy the included plugins into your simulator. No manual X-Plane network setup is required.

  • 01 Open the Package Inside the downloaded X-Plane folder, open the folder named plugins.
  • 02 Copy All 7 Folders You will find 7 plugin folders. Select all seven folders and Copy them together. Keep every folder and its contents intact.
  • 03 Open X-Plane's Plugins Directory Go to the main folder where X-Plane is installed on your PC, then open Resources followed by plugins.
  • 04 Paste & Launch Paste all seven copied plugin folders directly into X-Plane / Resources / plugins. Then launch X-Plane and open Simulator Station on your device.
Copy from: Downloaded Package / X-Plane / plugins / [7 plugin folders] Paste into: X-Plane / Resources / plugins
New, simplified connection: You no longer need to enter your device's IP address in X-Plane's Data Output settings, configure UDP port 49000, select Data Output indices, or follow the old video tutorial. Just install the seven included plugin folders as shown above. Keep your PC and mobile device on the same network.
Step 3

Instructor Screen Sharing Setup

Inside the X-Plane folder, you will find an executable file named BroadcastXplane.exe.

ATTENTION: Run this file ONLY if you are flying as a Pilot in a shared session with an Instructor. If you are flying solo or acting as an instructor, do not open this file. It is not required for normal application functionality.

If you are flying with an Instructor:

  • 01 Run the File Double-click BroadcastXplane.exe to open the broadcast interface.
  • 02 Enter Session ID You will see a text field. Enter the exact same Session ID that you and your instructor agreed upon and entered in the Simulator Station app on your devices.
  • 03 Join Session Click the "Join Session" button. This will instantly initiate the secure screen share, allowing your instructor to view your simulator live.
Step 4

Get Your Hoppie ACARS Logon Code

To unlock the full potential of the ACARS & CPDLC features, you need a personal Logon Code from Hoppie's system. Follow these simple steps to generate and connect it.

https://www.hoppie.nl/acars/system/register.html

* Click the button above to copy the link and automatically open the registration page.

  • 01 Register On the website, fill in your full name and email address. Answer the simple anti-abuse riddle (e.g., type "dog" for man's best friend), check the "I will behave" box, and click Register.
  • 02 Check Your Email You will immediately receive an email containing your unique ACARS Logon Code. Copy this code securely (check your spam folder if it doesn't arrive within seconds).
  • 03 Connect in App Open the Simulator Station app, navigate to the ACARS page, paste your code into the Logon Code field, and select your network (VAT or IVA) to establish the connection.

Flight Operations

Precision Performance Calculator (T/O & LDG)

Simulator Station features a highly accurate, airline-grade performance calculator that meticulously computes your Take-Off and Landing data based on real-world physics and conditions.

1. Take-Off Data Entry Input your specific aircraft type, gross weight, configuration, anti-ice status, and exact runway conditions (length, slope, heading, elevation). Add precise meteorological data including QNH, exact temperature, and wind direction/speed to ensure maximum realism and safety before departure.

2. Landing Data Entry (LDA) Prepare for a safe arrival by setting your landing weight, Autobrake settings, and reverser operational status. Combine this with the destination airport's elevation, runway condition, and live weather to dynamically calculate your required stopping margins.

3. Highly Accurate T/O Results & V-Speeds Hit calculate to generate impeccably precise V-speeds (V1, VR, V2) and Flex Temperatures. The app also determines your precise flap and slat retraction speeds (F, S, Green Dot). Best of all, the intuitive visual runway diagram displays exactly where on the runway your aircraft will hit these critical speeds relative to the total runway length (TORA).

4. Precise Landing Distances & Overrun Warnings Review your precise Approach Speed (VAPP) and Minimum Selectable Speed (VLS). The calculator actively compares your Actual Distance against the Factored Distance to tell you exactly how much runway will remain. If the runway is too short for your weight and weather conditions, a dynamic red OVERRUN WARNING ensures you abort the approach.

Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to the most common questions about Simulator Station.

Why is the app refusing to connect to X-Plane?

Ensure that both your device and your PC are on the exact same Wi-Fi network. Check the "Data Output" settings in X-Plane and confirm that you have entered the correct IP address of your mobile device, that the UDP port is set strictly to 49000, and that the selected Data Output indices are 3, 17, 20, and 21. Finally, ensure your Windows Firewall is not blocking X-Plane.

Why isn't my flight plan loading from SimBrief?

Ensure you have entered your correct SimBrief ID on the app's Connection page. Most importantly, you must click "Generate Flight" on the SimBrief website for your current flight before opening the Flight Plan tab in the app.

Does the map display live VATSIM and IVAO traffic?

Yes! Once you enter your VATSIM or IVAO ID on the Connection page, you can instantly view live network traffic. You can seamlessly toggle the traffic display on or off directly from the map's left-hand filter menu.

Can I use the Teleport feature while mid-air?

Yes, mid-air teleportation is fully supported. However, always ensure you configure an appropriate target Altitude and target Airspeed in the Teleport settings before executing the jump to prevent your aircraft from stalling upon arrival.
